Our Story

Passion for Perfection

At Mael Beauty, we believe that makeup is not just about changing how you look, but enhancing who you are. Founded with a vision to bring high-end editorial and bridal looks to every client, our team is dedicated to perfection.

We use only premium, carefully selected luxury products to ensure a flawless finish that lasts all day and night. From soft glam to bold artistry, we are here to make your vision come to life.

Meet the Lead Artist

Elena Mael

Founder & Master Makeup Artist

With a background in fine arts and over 10 years working in fashion capitals around the world, Elena brings a unique perspective to beauty. Her signature style is luminous, skin-focused makeup that highlights individual features rather than masking them.
